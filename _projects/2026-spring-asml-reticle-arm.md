For my M.Eng project, sponsored by ASML, I have been working since January 2026 on integrating an ASML reticle handling robot arm at Cornell so that students can use it safely.

## Mounting & Support Structures

- Redesigned the mounting approach and built support structures for the arm

## Safety Light Curtain

- Integrating an optical safety light curtain with the ASML sponsor to stop the arm when personnel enter its range of motion

## Documentation

- Writing operator documentation to help students learn to use the arm safely
