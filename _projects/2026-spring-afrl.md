Since March 2026 I have been a Graduate Researcher in the AFRL Aerospace ADVERSARY Laboratory at Cornell, working under the lab's Principal Investigator on a satellite testbed that evaluates space cybersecurity applications.

## CubeSat Structures

- Design CubeSat structures in CAD, including 3D-printed primary structures, for the lab's satellite testbed

## Attitude Adjustment

- Develop servo-actuated attitude adjustment that points CubeSats at one another for satellite-to-satellite communication
